# Accessibility

Nida aims to be usable by everyone, including people using screen readers, keyboard navigation or high-contrast themes.

- The settings panel and dashboard cards follow your Home Assistant theme, in light and dark mode.
- The interface is available in 10 languages, including right-to-left languages (Arabic, Urdu, Persian).
- Controls are intended to be reachable by keyboard and to carry text labels.

If you run into an accessibility barrier, please [open an issue](https://github.com/joohann/Nida/issues/new/choose) and tell us what you were trying to do. We will do our best to fix it.
